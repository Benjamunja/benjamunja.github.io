Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how SpotGenic ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the SpotGenic mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

Location Data

SpotGenic collects your precise and approximate location to show you photo spots near you and to calculate sunset and sunrise directions. This data is used in real time while you use the App and is shared with our advertising partners.

Photos

If you choose to add photos to location spots, the App accesses your device's photo library. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Device Identifiers

The App uses your device's advertising identifier (such as Google's Advertising ID on Android or Apple's IDFA on iOS) to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad performance through Google AdMob. This identifier is shared with Google and its advertising partners.

How We Use Your Information

  • App functionality — location data powers the core features of SpotGenic (nearby spots, sun angle calculations)
  • Advertising — device identifiers and location are used to display relevant ads via Google AdMob

Third-Party Services

SpotGenic uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and use your data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store personal data on our servers. Location and photo data is processed on your device only. Device advertising identifiers are managed by the third-party advertising networks according to their own retention policies.

Your Choices

You can revoke location access at any time via your device's Settings app. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.

Children

SpotGenic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
